## Tackle scene
- Defender now chases the ball carrier under server authority and moves faster than the attacker.
- Ball carrier remains restricted to the left side of the pitch.
- Larger tackle trigger radius creates exaggerated long-range tackles.
- Random tackle styles: slide tackle, flying kick and spin kick.
- Randomised flop motion variants.
- Press-and-hold D-pad movement for touch devices, sending continuous movement while held.
- Faster, smaller movement packets produce smoother iPad and phone controls.
